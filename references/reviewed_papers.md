# Reviewed Research Papers

This academic mini-survey examines five peer-reviewed empirical studies (2022–2024). The research findings described in this repository are **reported by the cited authors**, not results of new experiments conducted for this review.

1. **Aljedaani et al. (2022)** — W. Aljedaani, F. Rustam, M. W. Mkaouer, A. Ghallab, V. Rupapara, P. B. Washington, E. Lee, and I. Ashraf, “Sentiment analysis on Twitter data integrating TextBlob and deep learning models: The case of US airline industry,” *Knowledge-Based Systems*, vol. 255, article 109780, 2022. DOI: [10.1016/j.knosys.2022.109780](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2022.109780).

2. **Chinnalagu and Durairaj (2022)** — A. Chinnalagu and A. K. Durairaj, “Comparative Analysis of BERT-base Transformers and Deep Learning Sentiment Prediction Models,” *Proceedings of the 11th International Conference on System Modeling & Advancement in Research Trends (SMART)*, pp. 874–879, 2022. DOI: [10.1109/SMART55829.2022.10047651](https://doi.org/10.1109/SMART55829.2022.10047651).

3. **Bello et al. (2023)** — A. Bello, S.-C. Ng, and M.-F. Leung, “A BERT Framework to Sentiment Analysis of Tweets,” *Sensors*, vol. 23, no. 1, article 506, 2023. DOI: [10.3390/s23010506](https://doi.org/10.3390/s23010506).

4. **Zhang et al. (2024)** — W. Zhang, Y. Deng, B. Liu, S. J. Pan, and L. Bing, “Sentiment Analysis in the Era of Large Language Models: A Reality Check,” *Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: NAACL 2024*, pp. 3881–3906, 2024. DOI: [10.18653/v1/2024.findings-naacl.246](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.findings-naacl.246).

5. **Zhu et al. (2024)** — X. Zhu, S. Gardiner, T. Roldán, and D. Rossouw, “The Model Arena for Cross-lingual Sentiment Analysis: A Comparative Study in the Era of Large Language Models,” *Proceedings of the 14th Workshop on Computational Approaches to Subjectivity, Sentiment, & Social Media Analysis*, pp. 141–152, 2024. DOI: [10.18653/v1/2024.wassa-1.12](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.wassa-1.12).

**Source:** References reproduced from the author's final ENGI 9808 literature review.
